In traditional projects, we have many team members to perform different role. As the stakholders, they presents different perspectives' opionion and consideration. We needs the confirmation and approval from the different people to assure the responsibilites are fullfilled, and the requirements are confirmed to waive the project team's liability.
In current AI aided projects, we normally have only one developer to work on the project with EDPS skills. The developer presents all roles in the target business process. All information are fed in as described by roles, not by person's name. So we can focus on the collaborations of the roles and systems. The confirmation and approvals are not needed. It turns into build and clarification loop. The developer is responsible to reach out with shareholders for feedback and clarification to input the changes.
To handle both situation, I would like to introduce a switch of two mode, traditional and solo.
